# Product

Padel Kärnten is for Seb and the people he plays with. It answers one
question: **where in Carinthia is a padel court free when I want to
play?** It is good when:

- Opening it is enough. The courts show at once, and if they are older
  than five minutes it asks the booking sites again by itself, once.
- Picking a day, a time and how long to play shows every court that fits,
  with its price per hour and a link straight to its booking page.
- A booking site that fails costs only that venue, and the app says which
  one; the rest still show.
- It never shows who booked a court. Only free times leave a booking site.
- Only the people Seb invites get in, and only Seb invites, from the app
  itself. Withdrawing an invitation takes the person out again.
- It is gentle with the booking sites: one look every five minutes at
  most, however many people open it.

It does not book. Booking happens on the venue's own site.
