# Product

Padel Kärnten is for Seb and the people he plays with. Today it answers
one question: **where in Carinthia is a padel court free when I want to
play?** Next it answers the one after it: **when can four of us play, and
where?** It is good when:

- Opening it is enough. The courts show at once, and if they are older
  than five minutes it asks the booking sites again by itself, once.
- Picking a day, a time and how long to play shows every court that fits,
  with its price per hour and a link straight to its booking page.
- A booking site that fails costs only that venue, and the app says which
  one; the rest still show.
- It never shows who booked a court. Only free times leave a booking site.
- Only invited people get in. Today Seb invites, from the app itself;
  next, everyone already in may invite (Seb, 2026-10-28). Withdrawing an
  invitation takes the person out again.
- It says what is new, on the page Neu, once, and then leaves people be.
- It is gentle with the booking sites: one look every five minutes at
  most, however many people open it.

Where it is going (the technical vision, 2026-10-28): groups that form
themselves, private or open; each person says once when they can, in
half-hour spans; each group sees the moments when four of its members can
and a court is free; one proposes, four say yes, one books at the venue.
After the game a short rating, and later matchmaking from it.

It does not book. Booking happens on the venue's own site. It has no chat
and takes no money.
